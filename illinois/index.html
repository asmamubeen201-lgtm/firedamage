Quick Answers — Fire Damage in Illinois

What is the cost of fire damage restoration in this state?

Costs in Illinois typically range from about $1,500 for a modest apartment fire up to $50,000 for extensive commercial losses. Exact pricing depends on damage extent, property type, and specific services required, and estimates consider local labor rates and the older housing stock in cities like Chicago and Aurora.

Can I choose my own contractor?

You may choose any licensed fire-damage contractor, but verify their certifications, insurance, and experience with Illinois winter heating fires and older homes. Selecting a reputable team ensures proper board-up, soot removal, and odor mitigation, reducing future repair costs and safeguarding your property.

How fast is emergency response?

Emergency crews in Illinois operate 24/7 and aim to secure the property as quickly as possible after your call, starting with board-up and water extraction. Rapid action limits further damage, reduces structural harm, and preserves valuable contents while complying with local fire-safety rules.

What should I do in the first 48 hours?

In the first 48 hours after a fire, ensure everyone is safe, then contact a certified restoration service and your insurance provider immediately. Shut off utilities, ventilate the area, and avoid cleaning soot yourself to prevent further damage and health hazards.
