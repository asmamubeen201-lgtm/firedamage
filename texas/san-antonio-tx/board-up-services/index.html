7 Signs You Need Board-Up Services in San Antonio

These seven warning signs show when emergency board‑up is needed in San Antonio homes.

  1. Shattered windows or doorsFire or heat has broken glass or warped frames, leaving openings exposed to the elements.
  2. Burned or warped door framesHeat damage to door jambs creates gaps that compromise security and weather protection.
  3. Visible smoke damage on interior wallsHeavy soot indicates that fire has penetrated walls, increasing the need to seal the structure.
  4. Water from fire‑suppression systemsHoses or sprinklers have soaked openings, requiring immediate covering to prevent further water intrusion.
  5. Unstable structural elementsCharred studs or beams near openings suggest the building needs reinforcement before repairs.
  6. Candle or cooking fire remnants near openingsResidue from kitchen or candle fires often spreads to nearby windows and doors, demanding quick boarding.
  7. Electrical fire in historic homeOlder wiring in King William’s 1800s mansions can spark electrical fires, and damaged openings must be boarded to protect the historic fabric.
