7 Signs You Need Soot Removal in New Braunfels

These seven warning signs indicate that soot removal is needed after a fire in New Braunfels.

  1. Black streaks on wallsDark, oily streaks appear on interior walls and ceilings, showing soot has settled after a fire.
  2. Rancid smellA lingering, acrid odor persists even after the flames are out, indicating residual soot particles.
  3. Discolored paintPaint peels or turns yellowish where soot has penetrated the surface layers.
  4. Clogged ventsAir vents release a faint black residue, suggesting soot has traveled through HVAC ducts.
  5. Sticky surfacesTouching surfaces leaves a gritty, tar‑like film, a sign of wet soot that needs specialized cleaning.
  6. Health irritationResidents experience coughing or eye irritation from airborne soot particles after the fire.
  7. Grass‑fire soot driftSoot from nearby hill‑country grass fires settles on roofs and eaves, a risk specific to New Braunfels' moderate wildfire environment.
