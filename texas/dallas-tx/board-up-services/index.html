7 Signs You Need Board-Up Services in Dallas

These seven signs indicate a Dallas property needs professional board‑up after a fire.

  1. Exposed windows after a kitchen fireA cooking fire that breaks glass leaves openings vulnerable to weather and theft, requiring urgent plywood or steel coverage.
  2. Broken doors from dryer fireDryer fires in large apartment complexes can warp door frames, making board‑up essential to restore security.
  3. Charred wall openingsWhen flames burn through interior walls, sealing the gaps stops smoke spread and protects adjacent rooms.
  4. Missing shutters after lightning strikeSpring lightning strikes can shatter shutters, and boarding restores the building envelope promptly.
  5. Compromised roof edgesFire damage near the roof can leave eaves open, and temporary panels prevent water intrusion.
  6. Debris blocking entry pointsFallen debris can block doors, so boarding the remaining openings safeguards the interior.
  7. Electrical fire in pre‑1960 homeOlder homes, especially those built before 1976, often have aging wiring that ignites, and fire‑damaged openings in these structures need board‑up to meet Dallas Fire‑Rescue safety standards.

Quick Answers — Board-Up Services in Dallas

What does board‑up cost in Dallas?

The cost ranges from $300 to $900. Board‑up pricing reflects the material choice—plywood or steel—and the size of the openings, with Dallas contractors quoting within this range for emergency fire damage protection to secure the property within hours.

Does insurance cover board‑up services?

Insurance typically covers board‑up services when the fire damage is documented. Most Dallas homeowners’ policies include a loss‑of‑use endorsement that reimburses the $300‑$900 expense, provided the work is performed by a licensed contractor promptly after the incident and a written receipt is submitted to the insurer.

Board‑up crews can arrive the same day, often within 2‑4 hours on site. In Dallas, the fire‑rescue department notifies certified board‑up teams immediately after a fire call, allowing rapid deployment to secure windows, doors, and openings before weather or theft becomes a concern.

Does Dallas’ hail season affect board‑up timing?
