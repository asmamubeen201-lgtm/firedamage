7 Signs You Need Board-Up Services in Houston

These seven signs show when a board‑up service is needed after a fire in Houston.

  1. Broken or missing windowsFire can shatter glass, leaving openings that let rain and wind into the home, especially in older bungalows.
  2. Exposed doors or framesDoors warped or ripped away by heat create unsecured entry points that invite theft or weather damage.
  3. Roof or wall breachesBurned sections of roof decking or wall sheathing expose the interior to the elements and must be covered quickly.
  4. Visible soot and debris on openingsAccumulated soot on window frames indicates that the opening is compromised and needs sealing.
  5. Security alarms triggered by fire damageWhen alarms go off due to broken glass, the property is vulnerable and requires immediate boarding.
  6. Utility lines exposed after fireElectrical or gas lines left open after a fire pose safety hazards and need the area secured.
  7. Electrical fires in pre‑1970 bungalowsHouston’s older homes, like those in The Heights, often have outdated wiring that can reignite, making rapid board‑up essential to contain any lingering heat.

What does a board‑up service cost in Houston?

Does insurance cover board‑up after a fire in Houston?

Most fire insurance policies cover emergency board‑up as part of property protection. The insurer will usually reimburse the cost up to the policy limits, so keep receipts and document the damage for a smooth claim, and may require a prompt estimate from a licensed provider.

How quickly can a board‑up be performed in Houston?
