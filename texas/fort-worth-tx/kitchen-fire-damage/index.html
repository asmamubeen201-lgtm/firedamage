How quickly can a kitchen fire spread?

A kitchen grease fire can spread within minutes. Because hot oil ignites quickly and releases dense protein smoke, flames can engulf cabinets, countertops, and nearby appliances in under ten minutes, making rapid evacuation essential. The intense heat also damages structural elements, so professional assessment should follow as soon as the fire is out.

Restoring a kitchen after fire typically takes 3 to 10 days. The timeline includes soot removal, structural cleaning, odor mitigation, and any necessary appliance replacement, aligning with the 3‑10 day range noted for this service.
