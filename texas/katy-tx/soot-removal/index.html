7 Signs You Need Soot Removal in Katy

These seven warning signs indicate that a Katy homeowner needs specialized soot removal after a fire.

  1. Black streaks on wallsDark, oily lines appear on painted surfaces, showing that soot has settled and may penetrate the finish.

  3. Sticky residue on furnitureSurfaces feel tacky to the touch, a sign that soot chemicals have bonded with upholstery and wood.
  4. Unusual odor lingeringA lingering acrid smell persists even after the fire is out, suggesting embedded soot particles.
  5. Discoloration of metal fixturesLight fixtures and hardware turn yellowish, revealing soot deposition that can corrode over time.
  6. Smoke stains on windowsGlass panes show brownish smears, indicating soot migration through heat and air currents.
