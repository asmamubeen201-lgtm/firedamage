Quick Answers — Fire Damage in California

What is the cost of fire damage restoration in California?

Typical wildfire damage restoration in California ranges from $5,000 to $30,000. The final price depends on the extent of structural loss, smoke and soot penetration, and any specialized treatments required for odor removal or content restoration.

Can I choose my own contractor for fire restoration?

You may select any licensed contractor, but verify they understand California’s defensible‑space rules and have experience with ember intrusion. Confirm the contractor holds appropriate insurance, offers a detailed scope of work, and can provide references from similar wildfire projects.

How fast is emergency response for fire damage?

Emergency response begins as soon as you contact a 24/7 restoration service, with crews mobilizing within the hour when possible. Rapid arrival helps limit secondary damage, secure the structure, and start water extraction or board‑up work promptly.

What should I do in the first 48 hours after a fire?

Secure the property, stop water damage, and contact a fire‑damage restoration professional within the first 48 hours. Document losses, turn off utilities if safe, and avoid cleaning soot yourself to prevent further contamination before experts arrive.
