What a Full Restoration Costs

Most fire damage jobs are not one service — they are a stack of them. A small contained kitchen fire might need soot removal and odor treatment only. A whole-house fire needs board-up, water extraction, soot and smoke cleanup, odor removal, contents pack-out, and rebuild. That is why the national range is so wide: $3,083 at the low end, $52,031 at the high end, averaging $27,477 (Angi, September 2026).

One fact matters for every homeowner: 94% of restoration calls happen within 4 hours of the incident. The sooner mitigation starts, the smaller the bill — soot sets deeper and odor bonds harder with every hour.

What Drives Cost Up — and Down

Size of the affected area. Restoration is priced largely by square footage — $4–$7 per sq ft nationally (Angi, Sep 2026). One room is a different job than a whole floor.

Smoke type. Dry smoke wipes off; wet smoke smears; protein smoke from kitchen fires is invisible and the hardest odor to remove. Protein-smoke jobs take longer and cost more.

Water damage from firefighting. The water that saved your house can add $2,000–$6,000 in extraction and drying — and it is the step owners forget until mold starts.

Contents volume. Every item that needs pack-out, cleaning, and storage adds labor and time. Contents work alone can run $1,500–$10,000+ (restorenearme.com).

Structural damage. If the fire reached framing, roofing, or the electrical system, rebuild costs replace cleaning costs — that is where jobs move toward the top of the $3,083–$52,031 range.

Quick Answers — Fire Damage Costs

How much does fire damage restoration cost on average?

The national average is $27,477, with a typical range of $3,083–$52,031 (Angi, September 2026). Most jobs fall between $4 and $7 per square foot. Small, contained fires cost far less than whole-house fires with smoke and water damage.

What does fire damage restoration cost per square foot?

Fire damage restoration typically runs $4–$7 per square foot (Angi, September 2026). The rate rises with heavy soot, protein smoke, and water damage from firefighting, and falls for small, contained fires with light smoke.

How much does smoke damage cleanup cost?

Smoke damage cleanup runs $200–$1,200 per room (Angi, July 2026). Heavy soot can add $2,000–$6,000 to a job. Ozone treatment runs $200–$800 and thermal fogging $200–$600 per area; a whole-house odor job can reach $1,500–$5,000.

How much does soot removal cost?

Published estimates for basic soot removal run $400–$800 (PuroClean). A full soot-removal job — walls, ceilings, and contents across multiple rooms — typically runs $1,000–$4,000 depending on how far the soot spread.

Why do fire damage quotes vary so much?

Four things drive the price: the size of the affected area, the smoke type (protein smoke is hardest to remove), water damage left by firefighting, and how many contents need cleaning or storage. A contained kitchen fire and a whole-house fire are different jobs entirely.
