What It Is — How Pack-Out and Storage Works

A pack-out is an inventory job as much as a moving job. Here is the professional process.

  1. Everything is inventoriedEvery item is listed and photographed before it moves. This inventory becomes your insurance claim record.
  2. Items are sortedBelongings are sorted into cleanable, restorable, and non-salvageable — with you, not for you.
  3. Careful packingItems are packed by type and fragility, labeled by room, so they come back to the right place.
  4. Off-site cleaningSmoke-affected items are cleaned at a dedicated facility — ozone, ultrasonic, or hand cleaning as needed.
  5. Climate-controlled storageCleaned items are stored in a secure, climate-controlled facility until your home is ready.
  6. Return and resetWhen restoration is done, items are delivered back and placed by room, using the original inventory.

When You Need It

If any of these apply, your home needs a pack-out before restoration can start.

  1. The home is uninhabitableIf you cannot live there during restoration, your belongings cannot stay there either.
  2. Smoke-damaged contents everywhereWhen most of the home's contents absorbed smoke, they need off-site cleaning.
  3. Your insurer needs an inventoryA professional pack-out creates the itemized, photographed inventory your claim requires.
  4. Valuables are at riskIf the home will sit empty or open, valuables should be in secure storage, not in the house.
  5. Restoration needs empty roomsCrews cannot rebuild walls and floors around your furniture. The rooms must be cleared first.
  6. Water damage reached belongingsWet contents need drying and cleaning fast — left in place, they grow mold and become unsalvageable.

What It Costs

Pack-out and storage typically costs $1,000–$5,000 nationally. The range covers the pack-out day plus storage; longer storage and more belongings push the price up.

What drives the price up or down:

  • Volume of belongings — a full house costs far more than a few rooms.
  • Storage duration — weeks of storage cost less than months.
  • Cleaning needs — heavily smoke-damaged items need more off-site cleaning.
  • Fragility — art, antiques, and electronics need special handling and packing.
  • Access — stairs, tight doorways, and damaged floors slow the crew down.

Quick Answers

How long will my belongings be in storage?

As long as the restoration takes — usually weeks, sometimes months for major rebuilds. Storage is month to month, and you are told the expected timeline up front.

Is everything salvageable after a fire?

Not everything. Items are sorted with you into cleanable, restorable, and non-salvageable. Heavily charred or melted items usually cannot be saved — but far more can be restored than most owners expect.

How is the inventory documented for insurance?

Every item is listed and photographed before it moves, with its condition noted. That inventory goes to you and to your insurer, and it becomes the basis for your contents claim.

Who pays for pack-out and storage?

Pack-out and storage are usually covered as part of a fire restoration claim under a standard homeowner policy. Keep the inventory and all receipts — we provide both.

Can I access my belongings while they are in storage?

Yes — stored items can be accessed by arrangement. Tell the crew which items you may need (documents, seasonal clothing, tools) and they will be kept accessible.
