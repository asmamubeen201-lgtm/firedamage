What It Is — How Soot Removal Works

Soot removal is chemistry, not elbow grease. Here is the professional process.

  1. Soot type is identifiedThe crew tests whether the soot is dry, wet, protein-based, or fuel-oil — each type needs a different approach.
  2. Loose soot is removed dryDry chemical sponges and HEPA vacuuming lift loose soot without smearing it into the surface.
  3. Wet cleaning followsThe right cleaning solution for the soot type is applied to walls, ceilings, and contents.
  4. Surfaces are sealedCleaned surfaces are sealed with a smoke-sealing primer so residue and odor cannot bleed back through paint.
  5. Contents are cleaned or packed outSalvageable belongings are cleaned on site or packed out for off-site cleaning.

When You Need It

Soot spreads far beyond the fire room — through air, ducts, and on hands and feet. Look for these signs.

  1. Black film on walls and ceilingsA thin black or brown film, especially above doors and windows, is soot settling on surfaces.
  2. Soot webs in cornersCobweb-like soot chains in corners are a classic sign of soot traveling through the air.
  3. Blackened light fixtures and ventsSoot collects on warm surfaces like bulbs and registers — check them first.
  4. Soot on contents and furnitureIf your belongings have a black film, the soot spread through the whole living space.
  5. A sharp, acidic smellSoot smells acrid. The smell means acidic residue is still on the surfaces, still doing damage.
  6. Smearing when you touch itIf wiping makes the mark worse, that is wet or protein soot — it needs professional chemistry, not a rag.

Quick Answers

Is soot dangerous to touch or breathe?

Soot is acidic and can irritate eyes, skin, and lungs. Professionals wear respirators and gloves for a reason. Keep children and pets out of soot-affected rooms until the crew has cleaned them.

Can I clean soot myself with a rag and cleaner?

Usually not — wiping wet or protein soot smears it deeper into paint and drywall and can set the stain permanently. Dry soot can sometimes be lifted with a dry chemical sponge, but anything beyond a small area needs a professional.

Does soot come off painted walls?

Yes, when the right method is used for the soot type — dry removal first, then the correct wet chemistry, then a smoke-sealing primer before repainting. Painting over soot without sealing lets the stain and smell bleed back through.

How long does soot removal take?

Most homes take 1–3 days depending on how far the soot spread. A single room can be done in a day; a whole house with heavy soot takes longer.

Why does soot keep appearing after I clean?

Soot hides in ducts, behind fixtures, and inside porous materials, then redistributes through the air. A full cleaning includes the HVAC system and all surfaces — spot cleaning alone never finishes the job.
