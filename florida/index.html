Quick Answers — Fire Damage in Florida

What is the cost of fire damage restoration in Florida?

Fire damage restoration costs in Florida typically range from $1,500 for apartment‑scale repairs up to $50,000 for large commercial projects. These figures reflect the national price ranges for services such as board‑up, soot removal, and full‑property reconstruction, adjusted for the extensive lightning‑and hurricane‑related damage patterns common across the state.

How should I choose my own contractor?

Select a contractor who holds proper Florida licensing, carries insurance, and can provide verified references. Additionally, verify their experience with lightning‑induced and hurricane‑season fires, review third‑party ratings, and confirm they follow IICRC standards for fire‑damage restoration to ensure quality and compliance.

How fast is emergency response?

Most fire‑damage restoration teams in Florida aim to begin emergency response within a few hours of a 24/7 call. Rapid arrival is critical because the state’s frequent lightning and hurricane‑related fires can cause swift structural damage and smoke spread, so crews prioritize securing the property, board‑up, and water extraction as soon as possible.

What should I do in the first 48 hours after a fire?

In the first 48 hours after a fire, ensure safety, contact emergency services, and notify your insurance provider. After evacuation, document damage with photos, shut off utilities to prevent further hazards, and arrange for professional board‑up, water extraction, and soot removal to limit secondary damage while waiting for restoration specialists.
