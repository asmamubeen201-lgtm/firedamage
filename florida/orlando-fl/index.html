7 Signs You Need Fire Damage Restoration in Orlando

Here are seven warning signs that fire or smoke damage may be affecting your Orlando home.

  1. Blackened Walls or CeilingsCharred spots or soot staining on walls and ceilings indicate fire exposure, especially common after kitchen fires in downtown restaurants.
  2. Persistent Smoke OdorA lingering burnt smell that won’t dissipate suggests deeper smoke penetration into fabrics and HVAC ducts.
  3. Warped or Discolored WoodHeat can cause flooring, trim, or structural beams to warp or turn gray, a frequent issue in older College Park bungalows.
  4. Corroded Electrical FixturesElectrical components that appear melted or corroded often result from the electrical fires that affect 1920s homes.
  5. Stained or Bubbling PaintHeat and moisture from fire can cause paint to blister or develop dark stains, a sign to inspect for hidden damage.
  6. Clogged DuctworkSmoke particles settle in HVAC ducts, reducing airflow and spreading odor throughout the house.
  7. Lightning‑Related DamageFlorida’s status as the lightning capital means exterior wiring or roof penetrations from strikes can ignite hidden fires, especially on newer roofs in Lake Nona.

Quick Answers — Fire Damage in Orlando

What is the typical cost of fire damage restoration in Orlando?

Typical fire damage restoration costs in Orlando range from $1,500 to $50,000 depending on the extent of structural repair, soot removal, and odor mitigation. Prices reflect the national service ranges, with larger commercial projects reaching the higher end of the spectrum.

Does homeowners insurance cover fire damage in Florida?

Most Florida homeowners policies cover fire damage, including structural repairs and content restoration, subject to deductibles and policy limits. Insurers usually require documented estimates from licensed restoration contractors before approving payouts.

How quickly can board‑up services be deployed?

Board‑up services can be on‑site within 2‑4 hours after a fire incident in Orlando. Rapid board‑up helps secure the property against weather and theft while the restoration team prepares for further work.

How long does smoke odor removal usually take?

Smoke odor removal typically completes within 24‑48 hours using thermal fogging and ozone treatment. The timeline depends on the severity of contamination and the size of the affected area.

Is a permit required for fire restoration work in Orlando?

A fire restoration permit is required by Orlando building code for any structural repairs or major reconstruction after a fire. The permit ensures work complies with safety standards and is inspected before occupancy is restored.

What extra steps are needed because of Orlando’s lightning risk?

Because Florida leads the nation in lightning strikes, homeowners should have exterior electrical systems inspected after any fire. Lightning‑induced damage can compromise roof integrity and wiring, requiring specialized assessment before repairs begin.
