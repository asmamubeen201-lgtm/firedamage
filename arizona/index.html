Quick Answers — Fire Damage in Arizona

What is the cost of fire damage restoration in this state?

Costs in Arizona typically range from $300 for basic board‑up to $50,000 for extensive commercial wildfire damage. Exact pricing depends on damage extent, property type, and specific services required, and estimates consider local labor rates and the need for specialized equipment in desert conditions.

Can I choose my own contractor?

You may choose any licensed fire‑damage contractor, but verify their certifications, insurance, and experience with Arizona’s heat‑related and monsoon‑driven fires. Selecting a reputable team ensures proper board‑up, soot removal, and odor mitigation, reducing future repair costs and safeguarding your property.

How fast is emergency response?

Emergency crews in Arizona begin response within minutes of a 24/7 call, aiming to secure the property and start water extraction promptly. Rapid action limits fire spread, reduces structural damage, and preserves valuable contents while complying with local fire‑safety regulations.

What should I do in the first 48 hours?

In the first 48 hours after a fire, ensure everyone is safe, then contact a certified restoration service and your insurance provider immediately. Shut off utilities, ventilate the area, and avoid cleaning soot yourself to prevent further damage and health hazards.
